# Grandma's Arrest Warrent
Right out of the gate, as soon as everything is settled, the plot hook is simple.
Grandma is being arrested on high crimes against the realm of circles.
By a troop of royal guard, none the less.

- the players stow away on the guard carrier
	- they are unseen
	- they are caught
- the players fight the royal guard
	- the guards overpower the players, and are arrested
	(in this case, guards search and seize the house)
- the players remain docile
	- they go about their business until the next cargo run scheduled in `2d6` days.
	- they decide to investigate the home and see what's up with granny.
