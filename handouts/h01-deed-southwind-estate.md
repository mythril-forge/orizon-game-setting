# Deed to Southwind Farm & Estate
## Ownership Details
Full autonomy over Southwind Farm & Estate granted to the owner of this document within the purview of Imperial Law.
Ownership records, inspections, and other reports are detailed and signed off at the bottom of this deed.

## Archipelago Summary
The central isle of Southwind Archipelago is certified secure, and is suitable for regular development.
Surrounding isles are deemed too volatile; at the Empire's decree, do not disrupt or inhabit these lands.

## Natural Features
The island measures four acres of good land.
The predominant feature is a large lake generating freshwater.
It is home to a good quantity of fish.

Farm land is limited, but fertile.
Primary crops recommended: wheat, corn, blue beans, cliff-squash.

## Property Outline
- Main Estate
- Guest Estate
- Windmill
- 2 Storage Units

## Tax Agreement
An Empire-Owned air vessel will attend to your taxes every week.
The estate will give no less than one-third of its bounty in exchange for usage of this property.
Failure to produce minimum quantities may be grounds for legal action.

> You assume they are mostly from lords and ladies, among other bureaucratic officials.
> Naturally, you find the name of your grandmother near the bottom: Mabel Longriver.
