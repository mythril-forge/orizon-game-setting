# Grandma's Note
My dear grandchildren:

I am so sorry that I had to leave you in the way that I did.
I knew this would happen.
As much as I wanted to say goodbye to all of you, I couldn't.
I could not have any of you acting like you expected me to be taken away.
The officials suspect too much of me as it is.
I can't explain this situation all at once, but suffice it to say that I have only done what I have had to.
Now things have truly got out of hand.
I am being framed for something terrible, and they want me dead.

For now, don't come looking for me&hellip;there is not much you can do to help.
You must look after one another now.
With me gone, you have to take care of some things that I couldn't.

Your first order of business from here out is to deal with the estate.
Legally, the eldest child by blood will inherit the property.
I'd like you all to take care of any pending paperwork, and do whatever you think is best with our home.

I put a magic ring in this desk.
When you put it on, it will break a powerful enchantment that I have placed on each of you.
This was the way it had to be to keep you all safe growing up.
I know this must be hard - I've lied to you for your entire life.
Those lies end today.

With love, your grandma:<br />
&emsp;&mdash; Mabel Longriver

PS.
I left some cookies out on the table for you.
